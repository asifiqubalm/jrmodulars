## 🚀 Key Features

1. **Section 1: Sticky Navigation Bar**:
   - Refined JR monogram crest in champagne gold.
   - Transitions smoothly from transparent over the hero to dark solid glass with backdrop blur on scroll.
   - Desktop nav with active section indicator; mobile animated hamburger drawer with quick direct calls.
   - "Get a Free Consultation" CTA button.

2. **Section 2: Hero Section**:
   - High-impact luxury kitchen backdrop with marble island and warm illumination.
   - Eyebrow: `CRAFTING BEAUTIFUL LIVING SPACES`
   - Heading: `Designed Around Your Lifestyle.` (with gold italic styling)
   - Dual CTAs: *Explore Our Designs* and *Talk to Our Team*.
   - Animated scroll indicator and feature highlight ribbon.

3. **Section 3: Brand Introduction**:
   - 2-column architectural composition with luxury interior photograph and gold perimeter framing.
   - Core philosophy: *"Beautiful Spaces. Thoughtful Design. Better Living."*
   - Honest craftsmanship pillars (Bespoke space planning, precision factory finish).

4. **Section 4: Our Services**:
   - Three premium signature cards with subtle zoom on hover and gold accents:
     - **Modular Kitchens** (Island, L-shape, U-shape, parallel layouts, tandem drawers, BWR/BWP core)
     - **Modular Wardrobes** (Walk-in, floor-to-ceiling hinged, tinted glass sliding shutters, integrated LED)
     - **TV Units** (Fluted wood panels, floating media consoles, acoustic slats, ambient lighting)
   - Custom Whole-Home Woodwork banner.

5. **Section 5: Featured Interior Gallery**:
   - Filterable tabs: *All Designs*, *Modular Kitchens*, *Wardrobes*, *TV Units*, *Living & Dining*.
   - Responsive grid with hover overlays.
   - Interactive Lightbox with high-resolution imagery and direct inquiry buttons.

6. **Section 6: Why Choose Us (Craftsmanship & Process)**:
   - Zero fabricated reviews or fake metrics.
   - Authentic technical excellence: Boiling Water Resistant (BWR/BWP) plywood core, German soft-close fittings, zero-joint edge banding, and 3D spatial planning.
   - 4-Step transparent workflow: Site Survey → 3D Layout & Finishes → Factory Fabrication → White-Glove Installation.

7. **Section 7: Hyderabad Local SEO & Neighborhood Reach**:
   - Tailored specifically to homeowners in Bowrampet, Bachupally, Miyapur, Nizampet, Kukatpally, Kompally, Gachibowli, Tellapur, and Greater Hyderabad.
   - Search-engine optimized headings and structured data.

8. **Section 8 & 9: Contact, Location & Studio Map**:
   - Clickable primary (`8096582023`) and secondary (`9705522167`) phone numbers.
   - Direct Snake Park Road, Bowrampet address and Google Maps directions.
   - Interactive Consultation Form and direct WhatsApp chat trigger.
   - Embedded Google Maps view.

9. **Floating Dock**:
   - Quick WhatsApp button with pre-filled inquiry text.
   - Quick Click-to-Call button for immediate phone contact.

## 💻 Local Preview

Run any local web server in this directory:

```bash
# Using Python 3
python3 -m http.server 8080

# Or using Node.js npx
npx serve .
```

Open [http://localhost:8080](http://localhost:8080) in your browser.
